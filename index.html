01 — The Problem

The wallet is small.
The problem isn't.

The team's presentation identifies three everyday pain points: misplaced cards, time-consuming searches, and uncertainty about where the wallet was last placed.

01 / MISPLACED
💳

Important cards disappear

Student cards, access cards, bank cards and other essentials are often kept inside wallets.

02 / TIME
⏱

Manual searching wastes time

Looking around room by room can be difficult when you have no clue where you last left it.

03 / NO CLUE
📍

No last-known location

Conventional wallets have no built-in tracking feature, while separate trackers can be inconvenient to carry.

02 — The Solution

Make the wallet
findable.

TrackCard brings tracking into the wallet itself, keeping the concept slim, familiar and easy to use.

📡

Track location

Bluetooth Low Energy helps a connected smartphone identify the wallet’s last known location.

🔊

Hear the buzzer

When the wallet is nearby, a mini buzzer provides an audible alert to make the final search easier.

🔋

Rechargeable

The concept includes a compact rechargeable battery and charging/protection circuit inside the wallet.
