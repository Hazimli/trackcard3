01 — Workflow

Three steps
to find it.

01

Connect

The TrackCard concept communicates with a connected smartphone using Bluetooth Low Energy.

02

Locate

The phone helps identify the wallet's last known location within the tracking concept.

03

Buzz

When the wallet is nearby, the mini buzzer provides an audible cue to finish the search.
